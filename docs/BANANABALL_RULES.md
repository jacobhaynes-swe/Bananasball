# 🍌 Official Rules of Banana Ball ⚾️

Banana Ball is fast-paced, high-energy baseball reimagined by Fans First Entertainment. Games have a strict 2-hour time limit, point-based scoring, trick plays, and non-stop fan interaction.

---

## 1. Rule 1: Every Inning Counts (Point-Based Scoring)
- **Regular Innings**: The team that scores the most runs in an inning wins **1 Point** for that inning. If both teams score the same number of runs, no point is awarded to either team.
- **The Final Inning**: In the final inning (whether it is the 9th inning or the last inning before the 2-hour time limit), **every single run scored counts as 1 point**.
- The team with the most total points at the end of the game wins.

---

## 2. Rule 2: Two-Hour Time Limit
- **Hard Clock**: No new inning can start after **1 hour and 50 minutes**.
- If an inning is in progress when the clock reaches 1:50, that inning is finished.
- The inning that begins immediately before or at the 1:50 mark is designated the **Final Inning**, triggering the "Every Run Counts as a Point" rule.

---

## 3. Rule 3: No Stepping Out
- Batters may not step out of the batter's box during an at-bat.
- If a batter steps both feet out of the box, the umpire calls an **automatic strike**.
- If the batter steps out on strike three, they are called out.

---

## 4. Rule 4: No Bunting
- Bunting is completely prohibited in Banana Ball.
- If a batter attempts to bunt or offers a bunt, they are immediately **ejected from the game**.

---

## 5. Rule 5: Batters Can Steal First
- A batter may attempt to steal first base at any point during an at-bat on a passed ball, wild pitch, or any pitch that gets away from the catcher.
- The ball remains live, and the catcher or pitcher must throw the runner out at first.

---

## 6. Rule 6: No Walks Allowed (The Sprint)
- When a pitcher throws ball four, it is not a free walk — it triggers a **Sprint**.
- The batter takes off running around the bases as fast as possible.
- The ball is live, but **every defensive player on the field (except the pitcher and catcher) must touch the ball** before it can be thrown to a base to make a play on the sprinting runner.
- The runner can advance as far as they can until the defense successfully tags them or throws them out.

---

## 7. Rule 7: No Mound Visits
- Mound visits from the manager, pitching coach, catcher, or any other infielder are strictly forbidden.
- Pitchers must stay on the rubber and keep the game moving fast.

---

## 8. Rule 8: If a Fan Catches a Foul Ball, It’s an Out!
- Fans in the stands are active defensive fielders!
- If a fan catches a foul ball on the fly before it hits the ground or stadium seats/roof, it counts as an **official out** for the defense.

---

## 9. Rule 9: The Showdown Tiebreaker
If the point total is tied after the final inning (either 9 innings or after the 2-hour time limit), the game is decided by a multi-round **Showdown Tiebreaker**:

- **Round 1 (Pitcher vs. Batter + 1 Fielder)**:
  - Pitcher and catcher face the batter, with only 1 defensive fielder placed in the field.
  - If the batter hits the ball in play, they must attempt to score a home run before the single fielder retrieves the ball and throws it to the catcher.
- **Round 2 (Pitcher vs. Batter + 2 Fielders)**:
  - Pitcher, catcher, and 2 defensive fielders in the field.
- **Round 3 (Bases Loaded Showdown)**:
  - Bases loaded with a full defense in the field.
  - Any ball over the outfield fence is a walk-off grand slam.
  - Any ground ball or fly ball out ends the half-inning.
- **Sudden Death**: If still tied after Round 3, sudden death rounds continue until a winner is decided.

---

## 10. Rule 10: The Banana Ball Challenge Rule
- **Coach Challenge**: Each team is allowed to challenge one ruling on the field per game by throwing/shooting a challenge item on the field before the next pitch.
  - Categories eligible: Fair/foul, force/tag plays, catches in infield/outfield, missed bases.
  - If the call is overturned, the team retains their challenge.
- **The Fan Challenge Rule**: The fans choose one representative per night who has one opportunity to challenge an umpire's call on behalf of the crowd by shooting off confetti and holding up the "Fan Challenge" sign!

---

## 11. Rule 11: The Golden Batter Rule
- **Any Batter, Any Time**: Once per game, a team may send any hitter in their lineup to bat in any spot in the order.
- **Goal**: Allows a team's best hitter to step up when the game is on the line.
- Can even result in the same batter hitting in back-to-back at-bats in high-leverage situations.
